Our Process

From Vision to Celebration

Our comprehensive approach ensures every aspect of your destination wedding is meticulously planned and flawlessly executed, allowing you to focus on what matters most—celebrating your love.

💭
Phase 1 • 12-18 Months Before

Vision & Discovery

We begin with an in-depth consultation to understand your dreams, style preferences, guest list, and budget. This foundation guides every decision throughout the planning process.

  • Comprehensive vision consultation and style assessment
  • Destination research based on your preferences and guest needs
  • Budget planning and realistic timeline creation
  • Initial venue scouting and curated recommendations
  • Guest travel considerations and accessibility planning
📋
Phase 2 • 8-12 Months Before

Detailed Planning & Coordination

With your vision clear, we dive into the intricate details. Every vendor is vetted, every contract negotiated, and every element designed to reflect your unique style and love story.

  • Vendor selection, vetting, and contract negotiation
  • Guest travel coordination and group booking management
  • Menu planning with dietary restrictions and cultural preferences
  • Design concept development and decor coordination
  • Legal requirements research and documentation assistance
  • Photography and videography coordination
✨
Phase 3 • 1-3 Months Before

Finalization & Preparation

As your wedding approaches, we finalize all details, confirm arrangements, and prepare comprehensive timelines to ensure seamless execution of your celebration.

  • Final guest count confirmation and seating arrangements
  • Detailed timeline creation and vendor coordination schedules
  • Final dress fittings and beauty appointment coordination
  • Guest welcome package preparation and distribution planning
  • Weather contingency planning and backup arrangements
  • Final site visits and logistics confirmation
🎉
Phase 4 • Wedding Week

Execution & Celebration

Your wedding week is where all our planning comes together. We manage every detail behind the scenes, ensuring you can be fully present for your celebration while we handle the logistics.

  • On-site coordination and comprehensive vendor management
  • Guest arrival assistance and welcome event coordination
  • Day-of wedding timeline management and seamless execution
  • Real-time problem solving and immediate issue resolution
  • Ceremony and reception oversight with attention to every detail
  • Post-wedding cleanup coordination and departure assistance

What You Can Expect

Our meticulous planning process and attention to detail deliver wedding experiences that exceed expectations and create memories to last a lifetime.

  • Stress-free planning experience with dedicated personal support
  • Seamless travel coordination for you and all your guests
  • Carefully vetted vendors who understand and execute your vision
  • Flawless day-of execution with professional behind-the-scenes coordination
  • Unique personal touches that reflect your love story and style
  • Professional photography and videography arrangements
  • Exceptional guest experiences that create lasting memories
  • Complete peace of mind throughout the entire planning process
